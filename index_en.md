##  🔍**Research Focus**

- Planning and optimization of integrated energy systems  
  *Focus on multi-energy complementarity of electricity, hydrogen, and heat, promoting energy structure transformation and efficient utilization.*
- Robust/Stochastic optimization: advanced methods for power systems
